## 4. Proposed full-team run

**R0 — Orientation:** Share same sanitized three drafts, case-free, with one exact branch/commit ID. Collect independent initial critiques before comparison to reduce convergence bias.

**R1 — Independent evaluations:** Each lane returns concrete risks, counterexamples, architectural choices, unknowns, and a smallest test. Ask reviewers to distinguish what is observed, inferred, and proposed.

**R2 — Contradiction matrix:** THEIA records per-claim concurrence/disagreement; THOTH and PTAH assess their respective boundaries. Contradictions remain open until resolved with evidence; votes do not replace evidence.

**R3 — Red team:** Evaluate E2EE misrepresentation, private-data egress, cross-case leakage, model hallucination, falsified sources, prompt injection, unauthorized monitoring, malicious uploads, coercive UX, and mistaken-accusation risk.

**R4 — Operator disposition:** Return a succinct decision sheet: APPROVE CONCEPT / REVISE / HOLD / DECLINE, scope, constraints, named accountable owners, next permitted action. Canon promotion, implementation, funding, deployment, and data access each require their own subsequent gate.
